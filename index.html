Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our digital printing options, file mockups, and turnaround speeds.

What printing services do you offer?

SL Graphics offers standard B&W and color copies, custom business cards, flyers, pamphlets, booklets, coil-bound books, posters, stickers, labels, large format signs, storefront banners, and wedding invitation stationery.

What is digital printing, and is it right for me?

Digital printing assembles the images for print from a complex set of numbers and formulas. These images are captured from pixels, and the digitalised image is used to control the deposition of ink to replicate the image you would like to print. It is highly cost-effective for low-to-medium volume jobs and allows instant updates.

Can I get a custom price quote for my print job?

Yes, we provide instant pricing details in our booking form, but you can also use our custom request forms or call our Toronto store at (416) 531-4377 for a specialized wholesale volume discount.

Can you design or help me design my print job?

Yes. If you don't have a finished PDF design layout, our team of graphics designers will create it for you after discussing all the details during the booking verification phase.

Can you scan my hardcopy originals into electronic form?

Yes, we scan hardcopies and save them as high-quality PDFs or image files onto emails, CDs, or USB drives.
